Hospital Management

Doctors, patients, visits, diseases, and reports in one Odoo application.

Main features

Medical staff

Manage doctors, qualifications, mentors, interns, and assigned system users.

Patients and visits

Plan visits, track their status, record diagnoses, and preserve doctor history.

Disease classifier

Use a hierarchical disease tree with a convenient search panel and Ukrainian translations.

Reports and analytics

Generate A4 doctor reports and filter visit analytics by doctors, patients, diseases, and dates.

Role-based security

Access levels are inherited from Patient through Intern, Doctor, and Manager to Administrator. Record rules limit visits according to the current user's role.

Compatible with Odoo 19.0 · AGPL-3
